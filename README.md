# -Executive-Sales-Analysis-Excel-Dashboard


Project Overview

This project analyzes 1,800 sales records across products, categories, regions, customer segments, discounts, and delivery information. It focuses on understanding revenue generation, profitability, sales trends, and data quality using Microsoft Excel.

Tools Used


Microsoft Excel – data cleaning, calculations, analysis, KPI summaries, charts, and dashboard development.


Workbook Structure

Worksheet	Purpose
Cleaned Data	Cleaned order-level sales records and transaction attributes.
DASHBOARD	Executive overview with KPI cards, charts, and recommendations.
Monthly Trend	Monthly revenue, cost, gross profit, gross margin, and units sold.
Category & Region	Sales and profitability performance by product category and region.
Product Profitability	Product-level revenue, cost, gross profit, margin, and units sold.
Segment & Discount	Performance across customer segments and discount bands.
Data Quality	Summary of identified data-quality issues and the treatments applied.
KPI	Consolidated headline business metrics.

Data Preparation

The workbook documents the following data-cleaning actions:



Removed 12 duplicate Order_ID records, retaining the first occurrence.

Replaced 10 missing region values with Unknown.

Replaced 7 missing acquisition-channel values with Unknown.

Left 314 missing customer ratings blank rather than treating them as zero.


These treatments are recorded in the Data Quality worksheet.


Analysis Areas


Monthly revenue, cost, gross-profit, and margin trends.

Revenue and profitability by product category and region.

Product-level sales and gross-profit contribution.

Customer-segment performance.

Discount-band performance and discount amounts.

Order, unit-sales, and overall business KPIs.

Data-quality issues and their treatment.
